Privacy Policy

Keepsy: Warranty & Receipts — last updated November 20, 2026.

Keepsy does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no server, no account and no sign-in.

  • Products, receipts and photos you add are saved only inside the app on your device. The app never uploads them.
  • Receipt text is read on your device. Receipt images are not sent to any service.
  • The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add a receipt or a product photo.
  • Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push service is used.
  • The app has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.
  • If you share a product, the text and receipt go only where you choose to send them.
  • Your phone's own backups (such as iCloud Backup) may include the app's data, depending on your device settings.
  • Deleting the app, or "Delete all data" in Settings, removes everything it stored.
